# Contributing

Thank you for using Flashlight.

- **Bug reports** are welcome as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/georgio-sd/flashlight/issues): what you did,
  what you expected, what happened, and the lines of `/mnt/mailserver/log/instance.log` around it.
- **Feature requests** are welcome as issues too: describe what you need and why.
- **Code contributions (pull requests) are welcome.** Flashlight stays under its license (see
  [LICENSE](LICENSE)) and under one copyright holder. By opening a pull request you confirm that the work is
  yours (or that you may contribute it), and you grant Samson a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
  royalty-free and irrevocable license to use, change, publish, sublicense and relicense your contribution,
  under the project's license or any other terms. For a larger change, open an issue first so we can agree on
  the approach.
